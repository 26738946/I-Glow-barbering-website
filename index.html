About I-Glow

A standard, not a service.

I-Glow Salon & Spa was built on a simple idea: grooming should feel like an occasion, not an errand. Every fade, every beard line, every treatment is handled with the same discipline you'd expect from a tailor cutting a suit deliberate, unhurried, exact.

From our chairs in Kwara to your doorstep with our home service, we bring the same premium standard everywhere we work. Our mission is simple: give every client a reason to walk out standing a little taller.

Good To Know

Our policies.

Appointment Policy +

Appointments are confirmed on WhatsApp. Please arrive 10 minutes early — slots not honoured after a 15-minute grace period may be reassigned.

Cancellation Policy +

Cancel or reschedule at least 3 hours before your slot. Repeated no-shows may require a deposit for future bookings.

Home Service Policy +

Home service is available within Lagos on request, subject to distance and a call-out fee confirmed at booking.

Hygiene Policy +

All tools are sanitised between every client. Fresh blades and disposables are used where applicable, always.

Privacy Policy +

Your contact details are used only to confirm bookings and share offers you've opted into — never sold or shared.
